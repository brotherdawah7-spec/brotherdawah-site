Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 21, 2026

What we collect

When you authorize the Service, Instagram or TikTok provide an access token and basic profile information (such as account ID, username and display name). The Service also handles the videos and captions you choose to publish.

How we use it

This information is used only to upload and publish your own content to your own account, and to show the result of each upload. It is not used for advertising, profiling, or any other purpose.

Storage and security

Access tokens are stored encrypted in a private repository and are never published. Videos are deleted from the Service after they are posted.

Sharing

We do not sell or share your information with anyone. Data is sent only to Instagram (Meta) and TikTok, as needed to publish your content.

Your choices

You can revoke access at any time in your Instagram or TikTok settings, and request deletion of any stored data by emailing us. Revoking access immediately stops the Service from posting.

Children

The Service is not directed to anyone under 18.
