Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 24, 2026

What we collect

When you authorize the Service, Instagram, TikTok or YouTube (Google) provide an access token and basic profile information (such as account ID, username and display name). The Service also handles the videos and captions you choose to publish.

How we use it

This information is used only to upload and publish your own content to your own account, and to show the result of each upload. It is not used for advertising, profiling, or any other purpose.

Storage and security

Access tokens are stored encrypted in a private repository and are never published. Videos are deleted from the Service after they are posted.

Sharing

We do not sell or share your information with anyone. Data is sent only to Instagram (Meta), TikTok and YouTube (Google), as needed to publish your content.

Your choices

You can revoke access at any time in your Instagram or TikTok settings, or for YouTube at Google security settings, and request deletion of any stored data by emailing us. Revoking access immediately stops the Service from posting.

YouTube API Services

The Service uses YouTube API Services to list the videos on the operator's own YouTube channel, update their titles, descriptions, tags and privacy settings, and upload new videos to that channel. By using the Service you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy. The Service stores only an encrypted refresh token and a list of the channel's own video metadata (titles, privacy, view counts). This data is never shared, and is deleted within 30 days of a deletion request or of access being revoked. The Service does not use cookies or similar tracking technologies.

Children

The Service is not directed to anyone under 18.
