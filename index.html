Brother Dawah Tools

Brother Dawah Autopost: a private publishing tool used by the creator Brother Dawah (YouTube channel “Malik Fasahat Ullah-Brother Dawah”) to schedule and upload his own videos to his own YouTube channel, Instagram and TikTok accounts.

What it does

  • Uploads videos that the account owner has edited to the owner's own accounts, as drafts or scheduled posts.
  • Uses only official APIs (YouTube Data API via YouTube API Services, Instagram and TikTok), with the account owner's permission.
  • On YouTube: lists the owner's own uploads, updates their titles, descriptions, tags and privacy, and uploads new videos to the owner's channel.
  • Is not offered to the public and does not access anyone else's accounts.
