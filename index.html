Brother Dawah Autopost

A private publishing tool used by the creator Brother Dawah to schedule and upload his own short videos to his own Instagram and TikTok accounts.

What it does

  • Uploads videos that the account owner has edited to the owner's own accounts, as drafts or scheduled posts.
  • Uses only the official Instagram and TikTok APIs, with the account owner's permission.
  • Is not offered to the public and does not access anyone else's accounts.
